Conservation Objectives and Multiple-benefit Prioritisation Across Spatial Scenarios

Answer a few questions about your situation to see which COMPASS pathway fits and what you could expect to receive. It takes about two minutes.

An illustrative guide. COMPASS: Conservation Objectives and Multiple-benefit Prioritisation Across Spatial Scenarios supports transparent, revisable decisions with partners. It does not produce an automatic conservation plan or a single correct map, and the outputs listed are what a project would aim to deliver, agreed case by case.
